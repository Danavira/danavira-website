It has only been around 5 weeks since I arrived, so it's possible I'm still entranced by the unfamiliarity and novelty of the things around me, but I will try my best to give an unbiased review of my life in Japan thus far.

Today I turn 25, and I am blessed to be able to wake up to the subtle morning sounds of a calm neighborhood in Suginami, Tokyo. The smell of morning dew brought on by yesterday's rain, the refreshing cold air without polluted undertone. There simply aren't many things that can make me feel more alive than these, though I will almost surely take them for granted soon.

For those of you reading this hoping to move to Japan one day via work or other obligations, it is exactly as you imagined, provided you do not think of Japan as a dispenser of beautiful environments and communities where all you would need is money to partake. However, if the need for language, cultural fit, and respect were in fact in that imagination of yours, then you have successfully drawn a very accurate mental picture.
